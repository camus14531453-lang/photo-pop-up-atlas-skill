# One-pass project intake

Use the user's language. Prefill known answers and omit already-resolved questions. Present a single compact questionnaire, not a required interview. Every creative item may be answered “you decide.” Mark only true blockers as required. If the user already delegated all creative decisions, record defaults instead of asking again.

Suggested message:

“To avoid repeated background questions, please fill only what you know; ‘you decide’ is fine.
1. Purpose and audience: personal keepsake, video hook, public promotion, sale, or something else? Desired language and approximate play length?
2. Materials: which photos/audio/video should be used, what do they depict, and which details or exact words must stay unchanged? Upload or share the specific files I cannot yet access.
3. Experience: what should the player discover, solve, collect, or choose? Any visual references, must-haves, or things to avoid?
4. Devices and output: phone/desktop, full 3D or paper-cutout depth, private preview/public link/downloadable source, and any required existing host?
5. Permissions and limits: which supplied people/voices/materials may be transformed and included in the intended output; any privacy restrictions, usage-rights uncertainty, deadline, or budget cap?”

State that private preview is the default while publication scope is unresolved. Confirm purchases, third-party uploads, account actions, voice cloning and public release at the point required by the current authorization rules.

Turn answers into a short production brief:
- Audience, purpose, language, devices, play length, delivery scope.
- Source files, factual fidelity anchors, exact text, private/excluded material, rights uncertainties.
- Theme-specific core verb, objective, feedback, progression, end state, replay.
- Rendering contract and visual direction; audio's gameplay/narrative role.
- Accepted assumptions, authorization boundaries, missing dependencies, success criteria.

If inputs are missing, continue with a labelled neutral test scene and record which supplied assets will replace it. Handle supported attachment transfers directly.
